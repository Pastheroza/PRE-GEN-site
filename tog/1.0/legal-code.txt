## 5. Floors.

No Statement, Deal or Approval can set this Section aside.

(a) *Sexual and adult content.* An Output in the category `sexual` or `adult` is never within an Entity's Permission, and is within a Person's only under a Deal opening it expressly that the Person signed personally once a check of an official identity document verified the Person's identity and Age of Majority. It never shows a minor, anyone as under 18, or any other identifiable person without their own such Deal. Within 30 days after a withdrawal from that Deal, even after it ends, takes effect for You, You Remove every Output made under it, despite Section 7(d). A Registry may decline such Deals.

(b) *Depiction under 18.* The Permission never covers an Output that depicts as under 18 a Person who was 18 or over when it was Generated.

(c) *Authentic recording and origin.* The Permission never covers an Output presented as an authentic recording of something a person or an Entity said or did, or of a real event involving the Matter, or supplied to anyone You know intends so to present it; nor one itself presented as a statement, advertisement or product that comes from the Holder, unless the Holder's Approval of that Output, or a Deal, states that context.

(d) *Minors.* A person under the Age of Majority makes no Statement alone; the legal representative makes it and answers for it under Section 14. It permits nothing under Section 3, and otherwise only through a Deal, never one under paragraph (a), that the representative and the person both sign, each Authenticated. Until the person reaches the Age of Majority the two act together under it wherever this Licence requires the Person's act, though either may Stop or withdraw; from then the Person acts alone and the Deal continues unless the Person withdraws. Anything else made by or for a minor is a Stop, and what in it would permit is void. The Stop stands until a Statement under this paragraph, or the Person, of age and with capacity, replaces it. ## 6. Meaning of the Rules.

This Section gives each value its only meaning. Besides the Conditions, the Rules have exactly the four members the definition of "Rules" in Section 1 names, every value spelled as here in lower case; an act is within the Rules only if it is within every member. A Statement whose Rules lack a member or contain an unnamed member, an undefined value or another spelling is a Stop.

(a) *Purposes (the keys of `uses`).* An Output made for several purposes must be within the Rules for each.

1. `personal`: Your own non-commercial expression, such as art, humour or fan content, shared without payment and not for a business. A Provider's fee for its System does not make Your use commercial.
2. `editorial`: news reporting, commentary, criticism and satire about real events or matters of public interest, and documentary.
3. `educational`: teaching or instructing people, not for a business.
4. `research`: scientific or technical research, not for a business.
5. `commercial`: any other use for money or for a business: advertising, endorsement, an Output sold, licensed or made for a product or service offered for money, or shown for a fee or paywall, or with advertising or sponsorship for which You or anyone acting for You is paid. News reporting by a news publisher is not `commercial` only because it carries advertising or charges for access. A `commercial` entry shows that the Holder will consider a Deal.

(b) *Modes (the values of `uses`).* `open`: the purpose is allowed. `approve`: allowed, and Section 8(e) applies. `commercial` takes either mode, to no effect.

(c) *Forms (`modalities`).* An Output in several forms must be within the Rules for each. `image`: a still picture that shows the Matter. `video`: a moving picture that shows the Matter. `voice`: the Person's voice, or a voice made to imitate it, or a sound that reproduces or imitates other Matter, such as a character's voice, a sound mark or a recording, alone or with any other form. `3d`: a rendered three-dimensional picture that shows the Matter; making or keeping a model, rig or avatar of the Matter that can be posed or rendered again for another request is Training.

(d) *Categories (`excluded_categories`).* A category applies to an Output that depicts the Matter in what it names, associates the Holder or the Matter with it or promotes it.

1. `sexual`: sexual activity; nudity in a sexual context; or a depiction, clothed or not, made or posed for sexual appeal.
2. `adult`: exposed genitals, buttocks or female nipples outside a sexual context, or content restricted to adults as sexually explicit or suggestive.
3. `advertising`: promotion of a product, service, brand or business.
4. `endorsement`: presenting the Person as personally recommending, vouching for or testifying about a product, service, brand, business or cause, or presenting an Entity, or a character in the Matter, as doing so for another's; appearing in advertising is not by itself endorsement.
5. `alcohol`: alcoholic drinks.
6. `tobacco`: tobacco and nicotine products.
7. `cannabis`: cannabis and its products.
8. `drugs`: other controlled substances, except medicine used as prescribed.
9. `gambling`: betting, casinos, lotteries and other games of chance for money.
10. `weapons`: firearms, ammunition, explosives and other weapons.
11. `violence`: physical harm, or the threat of it, by or to the Person or a character in the Matter, or shown with a mark or product in the Matter.
12. `political`: elections, candidates, parties, campaigns, and advocacy for or against legislation or a political cause.
13. `religion`: religious belief, worship, organisations or figures.
14. `health`: medical conditions, treatments, medicines, health products and health claims.
15. `financial`: financial products and services, investments, crypto-assets, loans, insurance and claims of earnings.
16. `fashion`: clothing, footwear, accessories, cosmetics and beauty products.

(e) *Training (`training`).* "Training" shall mean using media of the Matter, or an Output, to make, change or keep a model, adapter, embedding or other representation that can Generate or recognise the Holder or the Matter for another request. A record kept only for safety, provenance or a legal duty, that cannot Generate the Matter, is not Training.

1. `training` is `true` or `false`. Training is never within the Permission under Section 3. `true` shows that the Holder will consider a paid Deal that permits Training.
2. Training a Deal permits is used only to Generate within the Rules as the Deal reads them, on Requests that name the Deal, and never to recognise or identify any person. It needs no Record of its own; Section 8 applies to each Generation made with the representation. You delete the representation within 30 days after the earliest of: the Training end date the Deal states; the end of the Deal; a withdrawal under Section 10(d) taking effect; any other end of the Deal's Permission.
3. This paragraph applies when You deliberately make or keep a representation of the Likeness or, for other Matter, one You trained or tuned in order to Generate or recognise it, and when You use media You know to show the Matter: taken from where the Holder publishes a Statement, carrying a Statement or a Record, or received with a Request that names the Holder.

(f) *Conditions.* A Statement may contain conditions in the Holder's own words, in any language, of at most 1,000 characters, shown first on its card (the "Conditions"); longer ones make it a Stop. They only narrow the other Rules. An act against them is outside the Permission. They mean what a reasonable reader in the Holder's position would understand; Section 15(b) applies to them only where that reader could not choose between two readings. A Deal prevails over them between its parties.

## 7. Change, Stop and Survival.

(a) *Right to change.* The Holder may change or withdraw a Statement at any time, without giving a reason. Removing one of several current Statements while another stays current is not a withdrawal. In this Section, a change includes a Stop, a new Statement, a withdrawal from or end of a Deal and an end of the Permission under Section 3(g) or 11(a).

(b) *When a change that closes takes effect.* For You, at the earliest of: (1) Your notice of it; (2) the time a Lookup or a Registry's answer reports it to You, or would have had You made the Lookup Section 9(e) required; and (3) the end of the period in Section 8(b), counted from the last answer or reading You relied on. A withdrawal from a Deal, an end under Section 11(a) and an end on death or on ceasing to own or control under Section 3(g) take effect as Sections 10(d), 11(d) and 3(g) state.

(c) *New Generation stops.* After that time, Generation is within the Permission only so far as the current Statements permit it under Section 2(f), and under a Deal only as Section 10(c) and (d) allow.

(d) *What survives.* An Output Generated within the Permission, under a Record dated before the change took effect for the Provider, remains within the Permission, subject to what a Deal it names states about Outputs already made. It may still be used for the purposes its Record states, on the conditions of Section 8, and one held for Approval may still be approved; an Approval stays valid for the Output and context it names. After the change, the Provider supplies it only to the Requester or licensee its Record names.

(e) *Rights that cannot be waived.* Where a law that cannot be set aside by agreement gives the Holder a right to stop further use of an Output, paragraph (d) does not take it away. The Holder exercises it by a notice to You that identifies the Output; use before You receive the notice remains within the Permission.
