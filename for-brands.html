For public figures, creators & brands

Your face is worth something.Protect it. Or monetize it. Your call.

Right now, AI companies generate content depicting real people, brands, and creative works without asking. PRE-GEN creates a third option between ignoring it and suing after the fact: get asked first — say yes on your terms, say no forever, and earn from every authorized use.

Register on PRAMPTA ↗ How it works

Register once. Every compliant AI model asks.

When your identity is registered, any AI provider that implements the PRE-GEN protocol must check your terms before generating anything depicting you. An opt-out — the strongest protection in the protocol — is checked before any license is even consulted: no license, no exception, no override. If you say no, the answer is no, cryptographically.

Not just protection — a revenue stream.

PUBLIC FIGURES & CREATORS

Your likeness is an asset

License your face, voice, or style the way you’d license a song or a photo. An influencer authorizes a campaign. An actor licenses their voice for a game. An estate approves a documentary recreation. Every license is scoped — channel, time window, product — and you set the terms.

BRANDS & TRADEMARKS

Partners get instant authorization

Instead of lengthy legal review per use case, issue a scoped license: this partner, this product, this territory, these disclosures. They get instant authorization from any compliant provider; you keep full control and a log of every use.

EVERYONE

The opt-out is free

Registration and the unconditional opt-out cost nothing — protection is not a premium feature. Licensing and monetization are there when you want them; saying “never generate me” is free forever.

What every authorized use leaves behind.

Each generation under your license produces a signed receipt tied to your decision — who generated, under which license, when. You can see the compliant ecosystem using your likeness, and every record is independently verifiable if a dispute ever comes.

Registration happens on PRAMPTA.

PRAMPTA is the protocol’s first commercial registry — where rights holders register and set terms. The protocol itself is open; the registry is where your rules live.
