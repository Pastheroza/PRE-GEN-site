Support the protocol

PRE-GEN stays useful only if it stays open and neutral. Support pays for specification work, test vectors, SDKs and a public conformance suite — not for any commercial registry.

  • Registration and opt-out stay free for the people being depicted.
  • The specification and vectors stay current, so anyone can implement PRE-GEN without paying anyone.
  • More SDKs and a conformance suite make “PRE-GEN compatible” something you can check.
