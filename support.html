Support ToG and PRE-GEN

ToG, the Terms of Generation, is the common agreement: one set of rules for what AI may generate, the same for AI companies, businesses, creators and people. PRE-GEN is the protocol that makes AI check those rules before it generates. Both are useful only while they stay open and neutral. Support pays for the licence, the specification, test vectors, libraries and a public test suite. It never pays for a commercial registry.

  • ToG-L 1.0 stays one free text for everyone, kept current, with plain-words summaries and worked examples.
  • Creating your ToG stays free for anyone.
  • Registering and opting out stay free for the people being depicted.
  • The specification and tests stay current, so anyone can build PRE-GEN without paying anyone.
  • More libraries and a test suite make “works with PRE-GEN” something you can check.
