Support PRE-GEN. Keep authorization open, free, and independent.

PRE-GEN is an open standard, not a product. It belongs to no single company. Your support keeps it that way — protecting the rights of artists, creators, and rights-holders in the age of generative AI.

Scroll for details

Why it matters.

Generative AI can already depict anyone — a person, an artist's style, a brand, a voice — without asking. The people most affected, independent creators and artists, are usually the ones with the least power to fight back. PRE-GEN exists to give them a way to say yes or no before anything is generated, and to prove it cryptographically.

For that protection to mean something, the standard has to stay open and neutral — free for anyone to implement, controlled by no single vendor. That independence costs money to maintain. That's where you come in.

Where your support goes.

Protecting rights-holders and artists

Building and maintaining the tools that let creators, performers, estates, and brands register their identity and control how it is used in AI-generated content — at no cost to them.

Running the open registry infrastructure

Hosting the verification API, the opt-out registry, and the externally anchored audit log that AI providers query before generating. Servers, security, and uptime are an ongoing cost.

Developing the open reference implementation

Keeping the Apache-2.0 reference code, SDKs, and documentation current — so any AI provider can integrate PRE-GEN without paying a licensing fee or depending on a single vendor.

Keeping the standard independent

Funding from the community means PRE-GEN doesn't have to sell out its neutrality to a single AI company. No privileged operator, no hidden agenda — the standard stays accountable to the people it protects.

Research, legal review, and standardization

Ongoing work to align PRE-GEN with the EU AI Act, GDPR, right-of-publicity law, and emerging regulation — plus research on detection and federation under the PRE-LEARN program.

Who this is for.

You don't have to be a company to care about this. If you're an artist worried about your style being cloned, a creator protecting your likeness, a developer who believes AI should ask permission first, or simply someone who thinks consent should come before generation — this is your standard too.
