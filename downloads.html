How to check

The file. The SHA-256 of the PDF you downloaded must equal the one shown above.

shasum -a 256 ToG-L-1.0.pdf
certutil -hashfile ToG-L-1.0.pdf SHA256    (Windows)

The text fingerprint. The hash of the legal text itself, the same in every copy and every format, and printed in the PDF. Download the plain text, licence or standard, and hash it the same way.

shasum -a 256 legal-code.txt ToG-RS-1.0.txt
