Pre-Generative Authorization Protocol

Version 5 · 12 October 2026

Before an AI system generates something protected — a person's likeness or voice, a character, a brand, a work — it asks the registry that speaks for it. The registry signs its answer, and anyone can check it later.

The core works the same for any protected subject. Each kind of subject gets a profile that says what may be asked and what can never be overridden: the first, likeness/1, covers a person's likeness and voice; profiles for characters, brands and works come next.

How it works

  1. 1
    Ask

    The AI service asks the registry before it generates.

  2. 2
    Signed answer

    The registry says yes, no or not yet — and signs it, for this request only.

  3. 3
    Only on yes

    The service generates only on a valid yes, and keeps the answer as its proof.

Four possible answers

Allow
A licence or the registry's published policy permits this use. The only answer that permits.
Not blocked
Nothing forbids it, and nothing grants it. Not a permission.
Review
The rights holder decides first.
Deny
Refused, with a reason.

Open to everyone

Anyone can run a registry or build it into an AI service, free, under Apache-2.0 — directly, with no intermediary. The whole core fits on one page. It works only where AI services choose to ask, and a signed answer shows what the registry said — not that a use is lawful.
