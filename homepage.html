Two parts

ToG · the licence

What is allowed

The Terms of Generation Licence: one text that says what AI may and may not do with a likeness, a brand or a work. A Holder applies it with a Statement at a registry and gets a ToG code.

Read the Licence → PRE-GEN · the protocol

How it is checked

Before an AI service generates someone, it asks their registry and gets a signed answer. It generates only on a yes, and the proof travels with what it made.
