How it works

Before an AI generates a real person, a character or a brand, it asks the registry that speaks for them, such as an agency or an estate. It goes ahead only on a signed yes.

  1. 1Ask

    The AI tells the registry who it wants to show, and for what.

  2. 2Signed answer

    The registry answers and signs it, so no one can fake it.

  3. 3Only on yes

    The AI generates only on a yes, and keeps the answer as proof.

The four answers

Allow
Yes. The only answer that lets the AI generate.
Not blocked
No decision here. Not a yes.
Review
The person decides first.
Deny
No, with a reason.

Two layers

PRE-GEN is the one thing every AI app and every registry adds. Selling licences is a registry's own, optional layer on top.

What a yes proves
What the registry said, and when. Not that a use is lawful. PRE-GEN works only where AI apps choose to ask.
