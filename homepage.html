PRE-GEN · an open standard

Terms of Generation Licence

One common text that says whether AI may generate a person's likeness, a brand or a work, and on what terms.

The PRE-GEN protocol puts it to work. Before an AI service generates a protected subject, it asks the registry that represents the subject and gets a signed answer. Only an allow permits generation.

Two parts

The Licence sets the terms. The protocol is how an AI service learns them in time, with a signature it can keep.

ToG-L 1.0 · the licence

What may be generated

A legal text, the same for everyone. A Holder applies it to their own likeness, marks or works with a Statement at a registry, and the registry issues a ToG code.

The Licence

PRE-GEN · the protocol

How it is checked

Signed requests and answers between AI services and registries, a signed directory of registries, and proof that travels with the output. Open specification, Apache-2.0.

How PRE-GEN works

  1. 1A Holder states the terms

    A person or a company makes a Statement under ToG-L 1.0 at one registry from the signed list. The registry issues the ToG code.

  2. 2The AI service asks first

    Before generating, it sends a signed request to that registry. The registry answers allow, deny, review or not_blocked, and signs the answer.

  3. 3Only allow permits

    The service generates only on a valid allow and keeps it. The signed answer shows what the registry said about that request; it does not settle ownership or legality on its own.
