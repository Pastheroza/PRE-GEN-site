What it is for

  • Protection from deepfakes

    AI services that use PRE-GEN check before they generate a real person. Without a signed allow from that person's registry, they do not generate.

  • Control of your identity

    You decide whether AI may use your face, voice, brand or work, and on what terms. You can change your terms or withdraw them.

  • Clear rules between people and AI

    One licence and open rules for people, AI companies and platforms. Every answer is signed, so anyone can check it later.

Two parts

The Licence sets the terms. The protocol is how an AI service learns them in time, with a signature it can keep.

ToG-L 1.0 · the licence

What may be generated

A legal text, the same for everyone. A Holder applies it to their own likeness, marks or works with a Statement at a registry, and the registry issues a ToG code.

PRE-GEN · the protocol

How it is checked

Signed requests and answers between AI services and registries, a signed directory of registries, and proof that travels with the output. Open specification, Apache-2.0.
