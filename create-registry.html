Create your registry

A registry speaks for real people, characters or brands. It gives each one a code, answers every AI that asks, and signs every answer. Anyone can run one, free.

The easy way: give this to your AI assistant

Copy the prompt and paste it into your AI assistant, for example Claude Code, Cursor or ChatGPT. It reads the guides, walks you through the choices to make first, tests a sandbox with you, and explains every step. When a choice is yours, it pauses that step and asks.

You are helping me create a PRE-GEN registry.

A registry speaks for the people it holds: it gives each one a code, records what they allow and refuse, and answers every AI that asks with a signed answer. Today PRE-GEN covers a person's likeness and voice (the profile likeness/1); characters and brands only where a registry chooses to list them. PRE-GEN is an open protocol, and anyone can run a registry on the same terms.

First, read these fully:
- https://www.pregen.org/llms.txt
- https://www.pregen.org/docs/registry.md (the complete guide)
- https://www.pregen.org/spec/PRE-GEN-CORE.md (the rules; where the guide and the rules differ, the rules win)
- https://www.pregen.org/spec/extensions/provider-identity.md (how a registry answers an AI app it has not checked yet)

Then work with me, step by step:
1. Help me decide what must be decided first, and explain each choice in plain words: our registry's name, our four-letter prefix, the domain it will run on, who operates it (legal name and address), and how we will check that a person is who they say they are and may decide for themselves.
2. Prepare the ground: tell me what I need (a server on our own domain with HTTPS, Python 3.10 or newer, a safe offline place for the registry's key), why, and roughly what it costs.
3. Follow docs/registry.md: install pregen-registry and create a local sandbox first, with fictional records only.
4. Test that sandbox beyond pregen-registry check, with fictional records: two jobs cannot both take the last permitted use; giving back an unused approval restores it; an opt-out blocks a fresh job, and note what happens to approvals already issued; and, asking as an app the registry has not checked, the answers for a private subject and for an unknown code are the same in every returned field.
5. Then, in this order and each only with my approval: where the registry's key is kept, and the public deployment on our domain (with --open-providers, so any AI service can ask without an account); our policy; pregen-registry check against the live HTTPS address until every check passes; the application for our prefix, which you prepare and I review; submitting it; and, once we are listed, checking that the signed registry list names us.
6. Add real people only after we have checked them, and only with what they agreed to. Never give a grant that any app may use (--any-provider) unless I choose it for that person.
7. Ask me before you accept any terms, store any secret, or publish anything.

As you go, explain each step to me in plain words, as if I am not a programmer. When a decision changes the scope, exposes data, or needs my approval, pause that step, tell me what my options are, and wait for my answer; meanwhile, continue work that does not depend on it, such as the sandbox tests. Selling licences is optional and separate: do not set it up unless I ask. The same goes for letting people connect their licences to AI apps (bind): it needs our own sign-in for people, which the reference registry does not have outside its sandbox.

What your assistant will do, in four steps:

  1. 1

    Install it

    The open reference registry, ready to run on your own server.

    pip install "pregen-registry>=0.4.0"
  2. 2

    Choose your four letters

    Every code you give out starts with them, like PG-ABCD-000001K (the last character is a check). One command creates your registry with its own key.

  3. 3

    Add who you speak for

    Check that each person is who they say. Give them a code, record what they allow and refuse, and the registry answers every AI for them.

  4. 4

    Get listed

    Send the application for your letters. Once approved, your registry is on the public list every AI app checks.
